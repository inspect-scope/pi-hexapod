# Printable parts

- **Frame and legs** come from MakeYourPet. Print them from
  [MakeYourPet/hexapod `STL/`](https://github.com/MakeYourPet/hexapod/tree/main/STL)
  following its README.
- [legs/](legs/) holds the leg parts printed for this build.
  `left-tibia.stl`, `right-tibia.stl`, `limiter.stl`, `micro-switch-tip.stl`,
  `servo-back-hole2.stl`, `shield.stl` and `tip.stl` are unmodified
  MakeYourPet files. `coxa_l.stl` and `femur_l.stl` are the left coxa and
  femur.
- [body/](body/) is the electronics enclosure that sits on the frame:
  battery floor, Pi and Servo2040 floor, screen roof and a snap-on skirt.
  Its [README](body/README.md) has the print list, fasteners and assembly order.

GitHub previews any STL in the browser. Click one to look at it in 3D.
